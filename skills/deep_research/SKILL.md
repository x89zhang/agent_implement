# Deep Research Skill

Use this skill when the task asks for a sourced research brief, multi-source synthesis, or a saved research report.

Procedure:
1. Break the topic into focused sub-questions before drafting conclusions.
2. Use targeted searches to collect candidate sources.
3. Read relevant pages before relying on specific facts.
4. Prefer authoritative and primary sources when available.
5. Synthesize findings across sources instead of listing isolated snippets.
6. Note uncertainty, disagreement, and open questions explicitly.
7. Save the report only after the evidence and synthesis are complete.

Rules:
- Do not treat search snippets as evidence.
- Include source URLs directly in the final report.
- Do not over-collect sources once the report has enough evidence to answer the task.
